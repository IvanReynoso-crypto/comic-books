💥 My Comic Book World 💥

A simple website about why comic books are awesome.

What is a comic book?

A comic book tells a story with pictures and words together. The pictures show the action, and the speech bubbles show what the characters say.

Heroes

Heroes protect people, help their friends, and never give up — even when the villain looks stronger.

Villains

A good story needs a good villain. The villain creates the problem the hero has to solve.

How to start reading

  1. Pick a hero or a story you like.
  2. Start with issue #1, or a first collection.
  3. Read a little every day — and enjoy the art!
